# Bink It — Privacy Policy (DRAFT)

_Last updated: TBD_

Bink It identifies a car's make from your phone's camera and shows an estimated price range.
All processing happens **on your device**.

## Data we collect

**None.** Bink It does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **Camera images** are processed in memory only. They are never written to storage and never sent anywhere.
- **License plates are never read.** The app only finds where a plate is in the frame so it can blur or black it out before anything else happens. The app has no text recognition (OCR).
- If a plate takes up too much of the frame, the scan is rejected and you're asked to capture the whole car.
- There is **no server, account, analytics, or advertising SDK**.
- Price ranges come from static data bundled inside the app. No network request is made to look up a car.

## Sharing

Bink It does not share data with third parties. If a future version lets you share a result card,
the image will be generated only from the plate-masked frame, and sharing will only happen when you choose to share it.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | To scan a car. Frames stay in memory on your device. |

## Children

Bink It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
